About Me

Hi, my name is Sujeet Kumar, and I am a PhD student at IIT Kharagpur in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, working on natural language processing topics such as machine translation, Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), and poetry-to-prose generation for Sanskrit with Prof. Pawan Goyal.

I am also a part of the Complex Networks Research Group (CNeRG), focusing on NLP, network science, and machine learning.

My work aims to advance the understanding and processing of Sanskrit using modern AI techniques, combining the richness of ancient texts with the power of computational models.
