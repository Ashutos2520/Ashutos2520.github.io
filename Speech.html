The genius of living mindfully and thinking from first principles! (15/09/2025)

Good afternoon to respected faculties, seniors, and my dear friends. As engineering students, we are trained to see the world as systems — each designed to optimize for something. A bridge is optimized for strength, a computer for speed, a circuit for efficiency. Now, what about capitalism? What is it optimized for? The answer is simple: profit. Nothing more, nothing less. I am not trying to make a point that capitalism inherently good or bad. It is just a system, with profit as its guiding principle. If something sells, it grows. If it doesn’t, it disappears. Whether it is healthy or harmful, meaningful or wasteful — the system doesn’t care. It only asks: does this make money? This nature of capitalistic system is at the core of a lot of suffering. Most of us, from childhood, are conditioned not to question the systems we live in. We are told what to do, and we follow. We rarely stop to ask the most basic questions. And because we don’t question, a lot of nonsense and suffering continues in the world. Let me give you a few examples. During mid to late 60's in United States, fake research was funded to make sugar look safe and fat look dangerous. The result? A world where obesity and lifestyle diseases skyrocketed. Not because people chose badly, but because they didn’t question the information given to them. Or think about products like tobacoo, alcohol even certain medicines. A tobacoo company won’t shut down because its product causes cancer. The system is designed that way. Profit first, everything else second. One of the people i admire is George Hotz. He is an excellent software engineer and an amazing thinker. I had once heard from him that "Democracy is based on consent". If someone convinces you to buy something harmful, or even convinces you to act against your own good, it is still considered your “choice.” That’s the power of persuasion in a system where most people don’t stop to think deeply. So what is the way out? For me, it lies in two words: mindfulness and first principles thinking. Mindfulness means pausing before consuming — not just food or media, but even ideas. It means not letting marketing campaigns think for us, because remember, they always think in terms of profit, not our well-being. When you are aware, you see pain more clearly. You understand suffering. You recognize manipulation. That awareness makes you more human, not less. And then there is first principles thinking. This is about questioning assumptions, breaking things down to their most fundamental truths, and then reasoning upward. Don’t accept habits or traditions blindly. Ask why. Because here’s the truth: everything in this world is a human construct. Money, corporations, brands, social norms — all of them are invented by us. They are not the laws of physics. So, Don’t just inherit opinions. Don’t just consume mindlessly. Question. Reflect. Rebuild. Because when you do, you stop being just another consumer in someone else’s system. You become the engineer of your own life. And that, I believe, is the true genius. Thank you.
